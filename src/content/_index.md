## What this project is all about

EU Moles is a source-first research directory for examining how Members of
the European Parliament vote and speak on issues that may affect Russia’s
interests. It brings together official parliamentary records, plain-language
vote context, and clearly labelled AI-assisted assessments to support
transparent, verifiable scrutiny.
