## Purpose

This project examines documented pro-Kremlin positions and possible influence
pathways in the European Parliament. It is not a substitute for journalism,
legal process, or the official register of Members of the European Parliament.

## What qualifies for inclusion

Each future profile or case entry should identify the subject, the exact claim,
the date, and one or more supporting sources. Prefer, in this order:

1. Official parliamentary records, voting records, declarations, and speeches.
2. Direct public statements, interviews, or publications by the subject.
3. Court records, regulator findings, or official investigations.
4. Reporting from established outlets that names its evidence and methodology.

Party affiliation, country of origin, and online association are context only;
none independently establish a pro-Kremlin position or covert relationship.

## Labels and uncertainty

Use precise labels such as **documented statement**, **recorded vote**,
**declared affiliation**, or **reported allegation**. Allegations must remain
attributed, linked, and clearly distinguished from verified findings.

## Corrections

If a page contains an error, missing context, or an outdated record, submit the
primary evidence required to correct it. Corrections should note what changed,
why, and when.

## Core sources

Use the [European Parliament full list of Members](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/meps/en/full-list/all)
as the primary reference for membership, national-party affiliation, and recent
activity. The directory's source dataset is a starting point; every case-level
claim requires its own directly linked evidence.
