## Core reference

The [European Parliament full list of Members](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/meps/en/full-list/all)
is the primary reference for current MEPs, their national parties, and recent
activity.

## Evidence register

No individual or party case files have been added to this demonstration site
yet. Before publishing one, record:

- the claim and the date it concerns;
- a direct link to each primary source or published investigation;
- the publication date and publisher;
- relevant response, correction, or contrary evidence; and
- the date the entry was last reviewed.

The country directory is an organisational dataset. It does not itself support
an allegation about a party or individual.
